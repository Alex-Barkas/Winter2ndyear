# MECH 330: Applied Thermodynamics II

## Grading Scheme
- **Traditional Assignments**: 5% (6 assignments, top 5 of 6 counted)
- **Jupyter Assignments**: 5% (3 assignments, top 2 of 3 counted)
- **Midterm 1**: 25% (Thursday, October 22, 6 PM)
- **Midterm 2**: 25% (Wednesday, November 25, 6 PM)
- **Final Exam**: 40%

## Traditional Assignments
6 assignments due roughly every other Monday by 11:59 PM, graded for effort/original content rather than correctness in detail.

## Jupyter Assignments
3 Python/Cantera notebook assignments modeling energy systems; may be completed with a fixed partner.

## Midterms
Both closed-book, with a formula sheet and steam tables provided.
