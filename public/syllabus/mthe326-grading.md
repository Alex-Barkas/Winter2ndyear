# MTHE 326 / MATH 326: Functions of a Complex Variable

## Grading Scheme
- **In-Class Quizzes**: 10% (12 quizzes, unannounced, best 10 of 12 counted)
- **Midterm Test 1**: 20% (covers complex numbers, analyticity, elementary functions)
- **Midterm Test 2**: 20% (covers series, singularities, residue calculus)
- **Final Exam**: 50% (cumulative, 180 minutes)

## In-Class Quizzes
12 unannounced pop-up quizzes at the end of lectures; the lowest 2 are automatically dropped, so no makeups or further accommodation is given for a missed quiz.

## Midterms
Dates/times/locations are announced within the first two weeks of term via onQ, once room bookings are confirmed.
