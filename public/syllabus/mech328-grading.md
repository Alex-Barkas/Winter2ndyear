# MECH 328: Dynamics and Vibrations

## Grading Scheme
- **In-Lecture Questions**: 5% (best 20 of all lectures)
- **Active Learning (ICAs)**: 20% (5 in-class activities)
- **Final Project**: 15%
  - Progress Report: 5% (Week 9)
  - Final Report: 10% (Week 12)
- **Midterms**: 30%
  - Midterm 1: 15% (Week 7)
  - Midterm 2: 15% (Week 12)
- **Final Exam**: 30%
  - *Note: You must pass the Final Exam to pass the course.*

## In-Lecture Questions
Submitted each lecture; only the best 20 count toward the 5%.

## Active Learning (ICAs)
4-6 group in-class activities completed during the Active Learning Session, due 3 days after the session.

## Final Project
A 2D planar mechanism design project, with a short progress update in Week 9 and the full report due in Week 12.
